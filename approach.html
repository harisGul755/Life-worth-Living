OUR APPROACH

Trauma-informed, compassionate care.

Every therapist on our team is trained in a wide range of therapeutic modalities, so care is shaped around what actually fits your history, your goals, and your family.

HOW WE WORK

Three areas of focus.

Individual Therapy

Confidential, trauma-informed support for children, teens, and adults working through anxiety, trauma, depression, and life's harder chapters.

Couples & Family Therapy

A guided space for couples and families to rebuild communication, trust, and connection with one another.

Telehealth

Secure online sessions for clients anywhere in Virginia, offered by every therapist on our team.
