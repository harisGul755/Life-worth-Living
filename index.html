QUESTIONS

Good to know before you start.

We're in network with Aetna, Anthem, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Humana Medicaid, Molina Complete Care, Sentara, and United Healthcare. Each therapist accepts their own subset of plans, and every therapist also accepts self-pay clients.

Self-pay sessions are $180 for the initial session and $160 for regular ongoing sessions. If we're in network with your insurance, we bill them directly at their reimbursement rate.

Yes. Every therapist on our team offers telehealth sessions for clients located anywhere in Virginia.
