ABOUT US

A collective of therapists based in Roanoke and Salem, VA.

Life Worth Living Counseling is a group of trauma-informed, compassionate therapists serving the Roanoke Valley in person, and clients across Virginia by telehealth, working with children, teens, adults, couples, and families.
